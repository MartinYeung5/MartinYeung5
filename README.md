### 👨‍💻 About Me

I am **MartinYeung5**, a senior computer engineer specializing in cryptography and Web3 + AI research, with extensive experience in smart contract (blockchain/Web3) research and development.

I am currently focusing on **Zero-Knowledge Proofs (ZKP)**, particularly the study and implementation of the **Plonk protocol**. I believe zero-knowledge proof technology will become the core of scalability and privacy protection for the next generation of blockchains.

After transitioning from traditional software development to Web3, I have been deeply drawn to the decentralized spirit of blockchain and the mathematical beauty of cryptography. Over the past five-plus years, I have devoted significant time to studying ZKP tools such as Plonk, Groth16, and Circom, deepening my understanding through the implementation of multiple projects.

**My goals:**
- Focus on digital security and privacy protection, building efficient yet secure tools
- Contribute to meaningful Web3 open-source projects (especially those related to digital security)
- Continuously accumulate hands-on experience within the Web3 ecosystem

Beyond coding, I enjoy reading cryptography papers, organizing study notes, and sharing what I've learned with the community. I welcome anyone interested in cryptography, blockchain, or AI development to connect and exchange ideas!
